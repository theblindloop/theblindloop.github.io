Nine categories of visual evidence

Browse 108 recorded question worlds by their measured image-support category. Each world has three original instances to try.

Categories describe where masking changes an inverse program’s answer or causes an error. Labels summarize measurements across scenes, not human attention or a pixel-level annotation of the displayed image. Examples have complete measurements, at least two-thirds category stability, and a matched request.
